# 📖 The Inkverse — A Literary Exploration Game

> *"Seven rooms. Seven literary worlds. Each one a different way of seeing."*

A browser-based literary game for curious minds who want to explore the world of literature — not through lectures, but through gameplay. No frameworks, no installs, no internet required. Just open and play.

---

## What is this?

The Inkverse is a single-file HTML game that takes you through seven rooms of a mysterious library, each representing a different literary world. Every room teaches you something real about how literature works — through challenges, not lessons.

It's built for people who are *curious* about literature before committing to it. No prior knowledge needed. By the end, you'll have encountered Dylan Thomas, Mary Shelley, Jorge Luis Borges, Chinua Achebe, James Joyce, and more — and you'll actually understand why they matter.

## Game Mechanics

- **Finish the Line** — complete a famous poem or passage
- **Hidden Meaning** — decode what a metaphor or literary device is really doing
- **Who Wrote It?** — identify the author or work from a passage
- **Write Like the Genre** — pick the response written in that genre's style
- **Literary Device** — name and understand the technique being used
- **Context & Meaning** — understand the historical or cultural stakes of a text

Every question comes with a detailed explanation — not just "correct/wrong," but the actual literary context, the term being taught, and the author's intent.

---

## XP System

Earn XP by completing rooms. Higher scores earn more XP. Locked rooms unlock as your XP grows — mirroring how literary education actually works. You earn the harder material.

Maximum XP: **350** (100% on all 7 rooms)
